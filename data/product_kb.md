# Tempus Product Knowledge Base

*Facts paraphrased from tempus.com and public regulatory filings, early 2026. Turnaround figures and test capabilities are updated periodically — confirm current published numbers before quoting a client.*

## [chunk: turnaround] Turnaround times

Tempus xF returns results typically within 7 days of specimen retrieval. Tempus xF+ returns in 7 to 9 days. Tempus xT Heme returns in 9 days from specimen receipt. Because xF and xF+ run from a blood draw, there is no wait for a tissue block to be located, cut and shipped, which is where much of the delay in tissue-based testing accumulates.

## [chunk: tissue] Tissue adequacy and liquid biopsy

Tempus xT CDx is a 648-gene tissue panel run with a matched normal sample. If the submitted tissue is insufficient for sequencing, the order automatically converts to xF liquid biopsy — no second physician order is required and no repeat biopsy conversation with the patient is needed. Tempus xF is a 105-gene circulating tumour DNA panel from a blood draw. Tempus xF+ is a 523-gene ctDNA panel that additionally flags variants associated with clonal haematopoiesis, helping distinguish tumour-derived mutations from blood-derived noise.

## [chunk: referral] Add-on tests requiring no new tissue

Tempus HRD is a homologous recombination deficiency score derived from DNA and whole-transcriptome RNA data already generated by an xT/xR order. No new tissue and no separate referral is required. HRD is directly relevant to PARP-inhibitor treatment decisions, most commonly in breast and ovarian cancer. Tempus IPS and Tempus TO are similarly derived from existing data with no additional specimen.

## [chunk: outcomes] Outcomes evidence

Tempus IPS (Immune Profile Score) combines DNA and RNA data to classify patients as IPS-High or IPS-Low. In Tempus real-world outcomes data, IPS-High patients showed higher overall survival on checkpoint-inhibitor regimens than IPS-Low patients. IPS is positioned as a more precise immunotherapy-benefit signal than PD-L1, TMB or MSI alone, each of which has known stratification limits. Tempus's oncologist network spans more than 7,500 physicians.

## [chunk: cost] Access and financial assistance

A financial assistance programme is available based on household income, with approval decisions typically returned at submission. Orders can be placed through the Tempus Hub portal, a paper requisition, or directly from an integrated EHR. Framing cost as patient access rather than a standing practice expense is usually the more productive conversation.

## [chunk: actionability] Algorithmic tests and subtyping

PurIST classifies pancreatic tumours as basal or classical subtype using RNA expression. Tempus validation found the classification stratifies survival outcomes and can inform first-line chemotherapy selection, with FOLFIRINOX benefit seen more consistently in the classical subtype. Tempus TO predicts the most likely tissue of origin from 68 possible cancer types using tumour RNA, useful in cancer of unknown primary.

## [chunk: incumbent] Trial matching and differentiation

Tempus clinical trial matching has been associated with roughly 96% of patients matching to at least one trial when clinical data is combined with Tempus NGS results. Tempus xR whole-transcriptome RNA sequencing identifies gene fusions across more than 100 targeted genes and splicing events such as MET exon 14 skipping and EGFRvIII, which DNA-only panels can miss. These are the areas where Tempus differs most from an established broad-panel incumbent.

## [chunk: bundles] Test bundling by specialty

Breast and gynaecologic oncology typically orders xT plus xR plus the HRD add-on. Pancreatic oncology typically orders xT plus xR plus PurIST. Thoracic and GI oncology typically order xT plus xR, since fusion and splice detection matters in those tumour types. Prostate, genitourinary, sarcoma and haematologic malignancy orders are more often xT alone.
